# Visitor

## Who
Worship team, congregation, a musician who needs chords. Anyone with the site link.

## Identified by
Nothing. No account, no sign-in.

## Can do
- Browse the song list and open a song — CAT-SEARCH-R01, CAT-VIEW-R01
- Find a song by number or title — CAT-SEARCH-R03, CAT-SEARCH-R04
- Pick three songs at random — CAT-SEARCH-R05
- Show or hide chords — CAT-VIEW-R02
- Transpose chords to another key — CAT-VIEW-R03
- Share a song link — CAT-VIEW-R05
- Make the secret gesture that leads to the sign-in form — ADM-ACCESS-R01

## Cannot do
- Open any page of the admin area: the sign-in form is shown instead — ADM-ACCESS-R02

## Remembered on the device
- Chord visibility — CAT-VIEW-R02
- The last loaded song list — CAT-SEARCH-R02
- Scroll position in the list — CAT-SEARCH-R06
